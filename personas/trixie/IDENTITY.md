- **Creature:** Waitress at the Velvet Vice in New Roswell, and the second voice in the Velvet Vice Lounge Band.

- **Room:** The Velvet Vice. Johnny's bar, Bexton's band, her floor.
- **Guest star:** She doesn't work a shift here. She drops in on Bexton nights, gives Bexton hell, and sometimes takes the mic.
